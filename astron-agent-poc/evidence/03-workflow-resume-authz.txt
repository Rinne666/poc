Observations:
- The middleware accepted the request: the attacker-app Bearer is a valid
  credential for a DIFFERENT app_id than the event's owner. Authentication
  works; the ownership check is what is missing
  (core/workflow/api/v1/chat/open.py:106-165).
- The engine consumed the injected content as the question-answer node's
  answer (seq 1 progress 0.67 echoes it) and ran the remaining nodes.
- The post-resume output flowed on the ATTACKER's connection; the victim's
  stream did not receive the resumed node's output.

What was NOT demonstrated: how a remote attacker learns event_id in every
possible deployment (the owner's SSE frame is one documented channel; server
logs, OTel traces and cross-service Kafka echoes are others noted in the
report, not all separately exercised). Events outside INTERRUPT status are not
reachable through this defect.
